## New

- The stream overlay is its own small mod, Mortar Stream Overlay, for Lethal Company and Stardew Valley. Mortar adds it to a profile while the overlay setting is on and removes it when the setting is off; the companion mod no longer carries it
- A Develop project shows the categories it declares, in the Mods list and the mod's details
- Mortar speaks Simplified Chinese, translated by volunteers on Hosted Weblate

## Changed

- Notices that something finished go to the notification list without popping up. Settings › Notifications › Something finishes well turns the pop-ups back on
- A mod's Dependencies list is alphabetical at each level
- Change version and Pin sit on the version's own line in a mod's details
- An Update all summary that still needs your choice pops up, even with success pop-ups off

## Fixed

- Show times in the console shows a time on BepInEx lines
- Switching games no longer shows the previous game's profile views while the new game's profiles load
