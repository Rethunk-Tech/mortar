Why Mortar?

How Mortar compares with each mod manager you might be coming from, with a source for every claim about it.

Mortar vs the CurseForge app

The CurseForge app is Overwolf's client for the CurseForge catalog (Minecraft, World of Warcraft). Mortar searches CurseForge too. Use Mortar for its other games, several sites, or an open-source app with no ads.

Mortar vs Gale

Gale is an open-source Thunderstore manager; Mortar reads its profiles. Use Mortar for games outside Thunderstore, or for Nexus and other sites in the same profile.

Mortar vs Mod Organizer 2

Mod Organizer 2 is the usual choice for heavily modded Bethesda games; Mortar reads its profiles. Use Mortar for games MO2 supports only lightly, native Linux, or one search across mod sites.

Mortar vs r2modman

r2modman is the long-standing Thunderstore manager; Mortar reads its profiles. Use Mortar for games outside Thunderstore, or for mods from more than one site.

Mortar vs Stardrop

Stardrop is a Stardew Valley manager; Mortar reads its profiles. Use Mortar to search for mods in-app, have SMAPI installed, or manage more than Stardew.

Mortar vs Vortex

Vortex is Nexus Mods' manager, with deep Bethesda tooling. Mortar works with Nexus too and reads Vortex profiles. Use Mortar for native Linux and the Steam Deck, several sites, or to skip the account.
