Start modding Potion Craft

  1. Download Mortar for Linux or Windows and open it.
  2. Pick Potion Craft. Mortar installs BepInEx 5 and makes your first profile.
  3. Open Browse, add the mods you want, and press Play. Each profile keeps its own mods and settings.

Share a profile

A profile for Potion Craft shared from Mortar is a link to mortar.rethunk.tech/potion-craft/p. Whoever opens it sees the mod list and imports it into Mortar in one click. The whole profile is inside the link, so nothing is uploaded anywhere.
