Shadows of Doubt

Mod Shadows of Doubt with Mortar, a free and open source mod manager for Linux, Windows and Steam Deck.

Mod loaderBepInEx 6 (IL2CPP), installed and kept up to date per profile
Mods fromThunderstore, searched together in Browse
Finds the game inSteam, or a folder you choose
ProfilesAs many as you like, each with its own mods and settings, shared as a link

Start modding Shadows of Doubt

  1. Download Mortar for Linux or Windows and open it.
  2. Pick Shadows of Doubt. Mortar installs BepInEx 6 (IL2CPP) and makes your first profile.
  3. Open Browse, add the mods you want, and press Play.

Share a profile

A Shadows of Doubt profile shared from Mortar is a link to mortar.rethunk.tech/shadows-of-doubt/p. Whoever opens it sees the mod list and imports it into Mortar in one click; the profile never leaves their browser.
