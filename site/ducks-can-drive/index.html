Start modding Ducks Can Drive

  1. Download Mortar for Linux or Windows and open it.
  2. Pick Ducks Can Drive. Mortar installs MelonLoader and makes your first profile.
  3. Open Browse, add the mods you want, and press Play. Each profile keeps its own mods and settings.
