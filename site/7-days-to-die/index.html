Start modding 7 Days to Die

  1. Download Mortar for Linux or Windows and open it.
  2. Pick 7 Days to Die. Mortar makes your first profile and keeps its mods apart from every other profile's.
  3. Open Browse, add the mods you want, and press Play. Each profile keeps its own mods and settings.

Share a profile

A profile for 7 Days to Die shared from Mortar is a link to mortar.rethunk.tech/7-days-to-die/p. Whoever opens it sees the mod list and imports it into Mortar in one click. The whole profile is inside the link, so nothing is uploaded anywhere.
