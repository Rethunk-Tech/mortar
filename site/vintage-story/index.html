Start modding Vintage Story

  1. Download Mortar for Linux or Windows and open it.
  2. Pick Vintage Story. Mortar makes your first profile and keeps its mods apart from every other profile's.
  3. Open Browse, add the mods you want, and press Play. Each profile keeps its own mods and settings.

Share a profile

A shared Vintage Story profile is a link to mortar.rethunk.tech/vintage-story/p. Whoever opens it sees the mod list and imports it in one click. The profile lives inside the link, so nothing is uploaded.
