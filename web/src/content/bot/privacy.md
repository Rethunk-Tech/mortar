# Mortar bot privacy policy

Effective 19 October 2026.

The Mortar bot is Rethunk.Tech's Discord app for the Mortar Mod Manager server, and it works only there. [Mortar's privacy policy](/privacy/) covers the app and [Discord's](https://discord.com/privacy) covers Discord.

## What it keeps

On our own machine:

- message text
- members' previous names, deleted when they leave
- the moderation record and transcripts of private tickets

In staff-only channels, copies of edited or deleted messages that moderators need for a case.

Anything else the bot acts on as it arrives and does not keep.

## Why

To run the server: requests to the bot, private tickets and moderation. Under the GDPR, that is our legitimate interest in keeping the server safe.

## Sharing

None of it is sold, used for ads or used to train AI. The only thing that leaves Discord is a bug report a moderator copies into a public issue on Mortar's GitHub.

## Your rights

To see, correct or delete what the bot holds about you, email [legal@rethunk.tech](mailto:legal@rethunk.tech). You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or your local data protection authority.

## Changes

We update this page and its date before the bot starts keeping anything new.
